# UPSC GS Mains Paper 2 (GS-II) Syllabus Hierarchy

This document outlines the structured 5-layer taxonomy of the GS-II syllabus, organizing Polity, Governance, Social Justice, and International Relations topics into logical section groups.

## SUBJECT: POLITY

### Section Group: Constitutional Framework & Evolution
- Indian Constitution- historical underpinnings and evolution
  - Making of the Constitution
- Features, amendments, significant provisions and basic structure
  - Amendments
  - Case Laws
  - Constitutional Morality
- Fundamental Rights, Preamble, DPSP, Fundamental Duties, etc.
  - DPSP
  - Fundamental Rights
- Comparison of the Indian constitutional scheme with that of other countries
  - Constitutional Comparison

### Section Group: Federal Structure & Local Governance
- Functions and responsibilities of the Union and the States
  - Emergency
  - Special Provisions
- Issues and challenges pertaining to the federal structure
  - Federalism
  - Union Territory
- Devolution of powers and finances up to local levels and challenges therein
  - Local Self Government

### Section Group: Organs of Government & Dispute Redressal
- Separation of powers between different organs
  - Executive Vs Judiciary
  - Executive Vs Legislature
  - Judiciary Vs Legislature
  - Separation of Powers
- Parliament and State Legislatures - structure, functioning, conduct of business, powers & privileges and issues arising out of these
  - Bicameralism
  - Parliamentary Committees
  - Parliamentary Privileges
  - Presiding Officers
  - Role of MPs
- Structure, organization and functioning of the Executive
  - Union Executive
- Structure, organization and functioning of the Judiciary (including Tribunals & ADR)
  - Alternate Dispute Resolution
  - Judiciary
  - Tribunal
- Pressure groups and formal/informal associations and their role in the Polity
  - Informal associations and their role in polity
  - Pressure Groups

### Section Group: Elections & Political Dynamics
- Salient features of the Representation of People's Act
  - Election Dispute and Disqualification
  - Electoral reforms
  - MCC
  - Political Parties

## SUBJECT: GOVERNANCE

### Section Group: Constitutional & Regulatory Bodies
- Appointment to various Constitutional posts; Constitutional Bodies (powers, functions and responsibilities)
  - Constitutional Bodies
- Statutory, Regulatory and Quasi-judicial bodies
  - Enforcement Agencies
  - Quasi judicial Bodies
  - Sectoral Regulatory Bodies
  - Statutory Bodies

### Section Group: Development Processes & Policies
- Government Policies & Interventions for development of various sectors (issues in their design, implementation)
  - Government Schemes and Policies
  - Structural reforms and Actions
- Development processes and the role of NGOs, Civil Society, and various groups and associations
  - Civil Society
- Role of Self Help Groups (SHGs) and microfinancing in development activities
  - SHGs
- Role of donors, charities, institutional and other external stakeholders
  - Donor Agencies

### Section Group: Accountability & Civil Services
- Transaparency and accountability (institutional and other measures); Citizens Charter
  - Citizens Charter
  - Transparency and Accountability
- E-Governance (applications, models, successes, limitations, potential)
  - E-governance
- Role of Civil Services in a democracy
  - Civil Services

## SUBJECT: SOCIAL JUSTICE

### Section Group: Vulnerable Sections & Welfare
- Welfare Schemes (Vulnerable Sections): Performance, Mechanisms, Laws, Institutions & Bodies
  - Children
  - Disabled
  - Efficacy of Welfare and Development Schemes
  - Women

### Section Group: Social Sector & Human Development
- Social Sector & Services: Issues relating to Development & Management of Health, Education & Human Resources
  - Education
  - Health
  - Human Resources
  - Miscellaneous (Social Sector)

### Section Group: Poverty & Hunger
- Issues and Challenges relating to Poverty & Hunger
  - Hunger
  - Miscellaneous (Poverty & Hunger)
  - Poverty

## SUBJECT: INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS

### Section Group: Neighborhood & Bilateral Engagements
- Neighborhood Relations: India & its Neighborhood-Relations
  - Neighbourhood

### Section Group: Global Geopolitics & Indian Diaspora
- Groupings & Agreements: Bilateral, Regional & Global Groupings & Agreements Involving or Affecting India's Interests
  - Bilateral Relations
  - Groupings beyond South Asia
  - Groupings involving Immediate and Extended neighbours
- Global Policies & Politics: Effect of Policies & Politics of Developed & Developing Countries on India's Interests
  - Energy X Foreign Policy
  - Geo-politics affecting India's Interest
  - Miscellaneous (Global Geopolitics)
- Indian Diaspora: Economic, Political & Social Impact of the Indian Diaspora
  - Indian Diaspora

### Section Group: International Organizations
- International Institutions: Structure, Mandate & Functioning of Important Agencies & Fora
  - International Funding Agencies
  - United Nations and its Agencies
  - WTO
