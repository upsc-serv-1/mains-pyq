# UPSC GS Mains Paper 3 (GS-III) Syllabus Hierarchy

This document outlines the structured 5-layer taxonomy of the GS-III syllabus, organizing Indian Economy, Agriculture, Science & Technology, Environment & Ecology, Disaster Management, and Internal Security topics into logical section groups.

---

### Section Group: Macroeconomics & Fiscal Policy
- Indian Economy and issues relating to planning, mobilization of resources, GDP, and monetary framework
  - Investment
  - Care X Monetized Economy
  - Digitalization of Economy
  - GDP
  - Monetary policy
  - Niti Aayog X Planning Commission
  - GST
- Issues relating to overall economic growth, sector manufacturing, and employment generation
  - Unemployment
  - Demographic Dividend
  - Economic Growth
  - Manufacturing Sector
  - Miscellaneous
- Inclusive growth and issues arising from it
  - Corporate Social Responsibility
  - Financial Inclusion
  - Inclusive Growth
  - Inclusive Growth X Capitalism
  - Inclusive Growth X Infrastructure
  - Inclusive Growth X Public Expenditure
  - Inclusive Growth X Sustainable Development
- Government Budgeting and fiscal management policies
  - Current Budget
  - Gender Budgeting
  - Fiscal Policy

### Section Group: Industrial Dynamics & Reforms
- Effects of Liberalisation on the economy; Changes in Industrial policy & their effects on industrial growth
  - Balance of Payment
  - FDI
  - LPG Reform and Impact
  - Protectionism and Currency Manipulations
  - Special Economic Zones

  - Labour Reform

  - Airports X Infrastructure
  - Renewable Energy
  - Transport
- Capital investment models and public-private frameworks
  - PPP X Infrastructure
